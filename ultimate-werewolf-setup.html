All Available Roles
Evil Team (Werewolf side)
WerewolfWakes each night to silently choose a victim to eliminate.
Alpha WolfA werewolf who, once per game at night, can convert a villager into a new werewolf instead of killing.
Wolf CubA werewolf. If the Wolf Cub is eliminated, the werewolves get to eliminate two players the following night.
MinionKnows who the werewolves are. Wins with them. Loses if all werewolves die before the game ends.
LycanA villager who appears as a Werewolf when seen by the Seer. Has no night power.
Village Team
SeerEach night, secretly learns the true role of one chosen player.
Mystic SeerLike the Seer, but also learns the alignment of a second player. Stronger version of the Seer.
Apprentice SeerBecomes the Seer if the Seer is eliminated. Until then, has no power.
Aura SeerEach night, learns only the alignment (good/evil) of one player, not their exact role.
MentalistEach night, learns whether two chosen players are on the same team or different teams.
RevealerOnce per game at night, publicly flips one player's card. Whether good or evil is revealed, the Revealer dies.
BodyguardEach night, protects one player from the werewolves. Cannot protect the same person twice in a row.
HunterWhen eliminated (day or night), immediately shoots and eliminates one other player of their choice.
HuntressLike the Hunter but can also use her shot proactively once per game at night before being eliminated.
WitchEach night, may save the werewolves' victim OR eliminate any player (one action per night).
MasonWakes with the other Mason(s) and recognises them as confirmed villagers.
DrunkStarts the game not knowing their role. At the start of day 3, the Moderator reveals their true role.
DiseasedIf eliminated by werewolves, the werewolves skip their kill the following night.
PrinceThe first time the village votes to eliminate the Prince, the Moderator reveals the role and the vote is cancelled.
Tough GuyWhen eliminated by werewolves, survives until the next day before being removed from play.
PriestCan bless a player each night. A blessed player is immune to werewolf attack for one night.
PI (Private Investigator)Each night, picks two players and learns if at least one of them is a werewolf.
SpellcasterOnce per game, silences a player for one full day — that player cannot speak during the day phase.
CupidOn night 1, links two players as lovers. If one dies, the other dies too. If both lovers are werewolves, they win with the werewolves.
Neutral / Third Party
TroublemakerOne night per game, stirs up trouble by calling for players to be eliminated the following day.
SorceressEach night, picks a player and learns if that player is the Seer. Wins with the werewolf team.
DoppelgangerOn night 1, copies another player's role. When that player dies, the Doppelganger activates their copied role.
Mad BomberWhen eliminated, all players sitting adjacent to them are also eliminated.
PacifistMust vote for a player to be eliminated each day — cannot abstain. Wins with the village.
CurseStarts as a villager. If targeted by the werewolves at night, becomes a werewolf instead of dying.
Cult LeaderEach night, recruits one player into their cult. Wins alone if all surviving players are cult members.
