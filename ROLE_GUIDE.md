# 🐺 Ultimate Werewolf - Complete Role Guide

## 9-Player Setup: Alpha Wolf Edition

**Evil Team (3)**: Alpha Wolf, Minion, Sorceress  
**Village Team (5)**: Seer, Doctor, Hunter, Mason, Villager  
**Neutral (1)**: Jester

---

## 👑 EVIL TEAM ROLES (Evil Team Wins)

### 🐺 Werewolf
**Alignment**: Evil | **Night Order**: 1st to act  
**Short**: Kill a villager each night. Wins with evil.

**LONG DESCRIPTION**:
The classic evil role. Each night, you and your evil teammates discuss who to kill (if you're not the Alpha Wolf or other special role). You must eliminate one villager each night. You win when evil outnumbers the village, OR when all investigative roles (Seers, Doctors) are dead and village can't find you. Key strategy: stay silent during day phase, blend in, and build trust. Never reveal your role unless forced.

**Night Action**: Kill one player (coordinate with other wolves)  
**Day Action**: Vote and discuss  
**Tips**: Agree on "code words" with your evil team during night phase. Build alliances with neutral roles if possible.

---

### 👑🐺 Alpha Wolf (MAIN EVIL FOR THIS SETUP)
**Alignment**: Evil | **Night Order**: 2nd (after base wolves) | **Weight**: -9 (Very Strong Evil)  
**Short**: Once per game, convert a villager to werewolf.

**LONG DESCRIPTION**:
You are the LEADER of the werewolf pack and the most powerful evil role. Like a regular Werewolf, you participate in night kills with your evil team. BUT you have a special ultimate power: **ONE TIME during the entire game, you can convert a living villager into a werewolf**, instantly turning them to your team and gaining an extra evil player.

**Strategic Timing**: Use this power early (Night 1-2) if you find a strong player to add to your team, OR save it for late game when village is strong. Once you use it, you're just a regular werewolf for the rest of the game.

**Example**: If a good player (like a Seer) has been helpful during day voting, use your conversion power on them to swing the game.

**Night Actions**: 
1. Kill one villager (with your evil team) EVERY night
2. Once per game: Convert a villager to werewolf (they wake up and join your team!)

**Day Action**: Vote and strategize. You can claim to be a village role.

**Tips**: 
- Don't reveal your conversion power early
- Pick a villager who seems intelligent and could influence day votes
- Convert someone who is TRUSTED (they're more useful on your team)
- If you're about to be voted out, use conversion as a last resort

### 🤝 Minion
**Alignment**: Evil | **Night Order**: 3rd  
**Short**: Knows werewolves. Wins with evil.

**LONG DESCRIPTION**:
You are a human helper of the werewolves (NOT a werewolf yourself). On Night 1, you learn the **identities of all werewolves** in the game. You don't participate in kills, but you help from the village side during day phase by pretending to be a villager. You win when all village and neutral roles are eliminated.

**Your Role**: Spy and informant for the evil team while blending into the village.

**Night 1 Exclusive**: You learn exactly who all the werewolves are (their real names/seats).

**Why You're Evil's Best Asset**: 
- You walk around during the day as a "trusted villager"
- You vote strategically to protect wolves
- If questioned about suspicious behavior, you had no role at night
- You can claim to be a Doctor or Seer to build credibility

**Example Game**:
- Night 1: You learn Seat 3 = Werewolf, Seat 7 = Alpha Wolf
- Day 1: Someone votes to eliminate Seat 3 (the Werewolf), you convince them it's a mistake
- You vote for Seat 9 instead (a real villager)
- At night, your wolf friends know you saved them

**Tips**:
- Don't be TOO helpful to wolves (suspicion!)
- Have a backup story: "I think Seat 3 is Jester, not Werewolf"
- The longer you survive, the more votes you can protect your team with

### 🔮 Sorceress
**Alignment**: Evil | **Night Order**: 4th  
**Short**: Each night, learn if target is Seer.

**LONG DESCRIPTION**:
You are an evil magic user with investigative powers. Each night, you pick ONE other player and learn if they are a Seer (any type: Seer, Mystic Seer, Apprentice Seer, Aura Seer). This is your ONLY power—you can't kill, you just gather information.

**Why It's Powerful**: You identify the village's main investigator and can tell your wolf team to kill them.

**Night Power**: Point to one player → Learn: "Seer?" (Yes/No)
- YES = They are a Seer (any type) → Report to evil team to kill them
- NO = They are NOT a Seer → Might be Doctor, Hunter, Villager, etc.

**Example Game**:
- Night 1: Check Seat 2 → "YES, SEER!" → Tell wolves to kill Seat 2
- Night 2: Check Seat 5 → "NO" → Check someone else

**Tips**:
- Prioritize checking high-influence players (who speak well during day)
- Check players who claim to be something else (Doctors claiming they "protected" someone)
- Never reveal your information publicly—only tell your werewolf allies
- If caught, claim you're a Doctor or low-value role

---

### 🔮‍♂️ Sorcerer (Alternative Evil Option)
**Alignment**: Evil | **Night Order**: 27th  
**Short**: Evil seer: learn role (werewolf only), other seer, or nothing.

**LONG DESCRIPTION**:
You are an evil twist on the regular Seer. Each night, you choose one of three options:
1. **Learn if target is a Werewolf** (exact werewolf role? Yes/No)
2. **Learn if target is another Seer** (any seer type? Yes/No)
3. **Learn NOTHING** (blank information, useful for confusion)

You're weaker than Sorceress (slower night order) but more flexible. Pick wisely which piece of info helps your team most that night.

## 💚 VILLAGE TEAM ROLES (Village Wins)

### 🔍 Seer
**Alignment**: Village | **Night Order**: 5th  
**Short**: Each night, learn one player's true role.

**LONG DESCRIPTION**:
You are the PRIMARY detective role for the village. Each night (starting Night 1), you point at any other player and learn their EXACT role (e.g., "Werewolf", "Seer", "Minion", "Villager"). You WIN when all evil and neutral roles are dead.

**YOUR SUPERPOWER**: You know the exact role of at least one person each morning. Use this to guide day votes.

**Night Power**: Check one player → Learn their TRUE ROLE
- "Werewolf" = They're evil (probably!)
- "Seer" = Another detective! Work together.
- "Minion" = They're evil but pretending to be village
- "Villager" = They're clean/innocent
- "Hunter" = Village ally with revenge power

**Day Strategy**:
- If you find a Werewolf, you know for certain they're evil → Vote them out
- If you find another Seer, quietly team up and cross-check
- If you find a Villager, don't reveal it (no point tipping off wolves)
- NEVER reveal you're Seer during day phase (wolves will kill you Night 2)

**Example Day Speech** (without revealing Seer):
- "I think Seat 3 is suspicious. I have information. Please eliminate them."
- Then Seat 3 gets voted out, and wolves DON'T know you're Seer yet

**Tips**:
- Check players who are TOO QUIET (wolves blend in by staying silent)
- Check players making BOLD CLAIMS ("I'm the Doctor!")
- Check the MINION if you find a Werewolf (minions are near wolves)
- Once you reveal you're a Seer in endgame, werewolves hunt you immediately

**Weaknesses**:
- Alpha Wolf can convert you to werewolf (switch teams!)
- Sorceress learns when you're a Seer and tells wolves to kill you
- You die to night kills like everyone else

### ⚕️ Doctor
**Alignment**: Village | **Night Order**: 21st  
**Short**: Protect one player each night from death.

**LONG DESCRIPTION**:
You are the village's bodyguard. Each night, you choose ONE other player to protect. If that player is targeted by werewolves for death, they are SAVED and survive the night. You can protect the same person multiple nights, or different people. You WIN when all evil and neutral roles are dead.

**Night Power**: Guard one player → If wolves kill them, they survive
- You can protect anyone: yourself, other village, even a werewolf (but they still lose!)
- You can protect the same person every night (e.g., protect the Seer if you know who they are)
- You can protect someone different each night

**Strategy**:
- If you're the only defensive role, you're a target (wolves want you dead)
- Protect high-value roles: Seers, Hunters, other Doctors if paired
- Never say who you protected publicly (helps wolves plan next kill)
- If there's a Bodyguard too, coordinate: one guards position 1-3, other guards 4-6

**Example Night**:
- Night 1: Wolf team targets Seat 5 (Seer)
- You guarded Seat 5 → Seer survives! Wolves are shocked!
- Wolf team targets someone else that night

**Tips**:
- Play quietly so wolves don't know you exist
- Protect yourself if you think you're about to be found out
- In endgame (1v1 scenarios), predict who wolves will kill next
- If you protect successfully, reveal it LATE in the game (to prevent early elimination)

**Weakness**: If you protect the wrong person, someone else dies. Wolves might trick you into protecting the wrong target.

### 🏹 Hunter
**Alignment**: Village | **Night Order**: N/A (Day Power)  
**Short**: When killed, take someone down with you.

**LONG DESCRIPTION**:
You are a warrior/soldier for the village. You have NO night power, but you have an EXPLOSIVE revenge ability: **If you are eliminated during day voting OR killed by werewolves at night, you get to choose ONE person to take down with you before you die.**

This is a suicide bomb power—you die anyway, but you drag one wolf (or suspect) with you.

**Your Advantage**: Wolves FEAR eliminating you because they lose 2 players (you + your revenge target).

**Example Game**:
- Day 3: Wolves vote out the Hunter
- Before the Hunter is eliminated, they say: "I'm taking Seat 4 with me!"
- Seat 4 dies too (whether they're a wolf or not)
- Net result: Village loses 1, Evil loses 2 → Village gains advantage

**Day Power**: When voted out → Choose one player to die with you

**Night Power**: None, you sleep like a regular villager

**Tips**:
- Don't reveal you're the Hunter early (wolves will scheme to avoid you)
- Hold your revenge card close to your chest
- If you KNOW Seat 3 is a werewolf, get them to vote you out, then revenge on them
- If you're uncertain, revenge on the person leading your elimination vote (likely a wolf manipulating)
- Use your death strategically: better to die taking a wolf than a villager

**Weakness**: Wolves might vote someone else out first, never touching you. Or they might coordinate a day-vote against someone else and let the village handle you naturally.

### ⚕️ Bodyguard
**Alignment**: Village | **Night Order**: 12th  
**Short**: Protect one from werewolves each night.

**LONG DESCRIPTION**:
Similar to Doctor, but slightly different mechanics. Each night, you choose ONE person to be your "bodyguard assignment." If werewolves try to kill that person, the kill is BLOCKED. However:

**Key Difference from Doctor**:
- You can protect the same person every night
- Bodyguard + Doctor can stack (if both protect same target, extra safe!)
- You might have limits (some games: can't protect same person 2 nights in a row)

**Strategic Role**: You're typically paired with a Doctor in larger games to provide redundancy. One guards odd-numbered players, the other guards even-numbered players.

**Night Power**: Guard one player → Block werewolf kill on them

**Tips**:
- If you know who the Seer is, guard them every night
- If you don't know, guard whoever is being investigated or seems important
- In pairs: divide and conquer
- Claim to be a "Priest" if questioned (similar vibe, less suspicious)

### 🪨 Mason
**Alignment**: Village | **Night Order**: N/A  
**Short**: Know other Masons.

**LONG DESCRIPTION**:
You are a secret member of a village society/organization. If there are TWO Masons in the game, you learn who the other Mason is on Night 1. If there's only ONE Mason (you), you get no information.

**Mechanics**:
- Night 1: You learn the IDENTITY of the other Mason (if one exists)
- If there's 2 Masons: You can trust each other completely
- If there's 1 Mason: You're just a regular Villager (no power)
- If there's 3+ Masons: Some games allow this (very rare)

**Strategy**:
- With another Mason: You two have a confirmed alliance to execute day votes together
- Without another Mason: Keep quiet and act like a regular Villager
- With another Mason: Don't reveal too early or wolves will focus-kill one of you

**Example Night 1**:
- Mason (Seat 3) learns: Mason is at Seat 7
- Seats 3 & 7 now have a secret partnership
- Seat 3 can whisper to Seat 7: "I'm Mason, you're Mason, let's vote together"
- They vote as a 2-person bloc, making them very hard to eliminate

**Strength**: Confirmed pair in an uncertain world  
**Weakness**: If one Mason dies, the other Mason is outed (wolves focus the survivor)

### 👨‍🌾 Villager
**Alignment**: Village | **Night Order**: N/A  
**Short**: Find and eliminate werewolves.

**LONG DESCRIPTION**:
You are a regular villager with NO SPECIAL POWERS. You have no night action, no investigation, no protection—just pure voting power during the day phase.

**Your Strength**: There are MANY of you. In a 9-player game with 5 village roles, you might have 0-2 Villagers (the rest are Seer, Doctor, Hunter, Mason). Villagers are the "body count" of the village army.

**Day Action**: Vote and discuss  
**Night Action**: SLEEP (nothing happens)  
**Win Condition**: All evil and neutral roles dead

**Strategy**:
- Listen carefully during day phase for clues
- Support the strong speakers (usually Seer or Detective)
- If you have a Seer hinting someone is evil, vote that person out
- Use pure logic: "Who's saying contradictory things?"
- Build alliances with other villagers

**Why Villagers Exist**: 
- To represent the common person
- To add voting power (5 villagers voting is a majority)
- To provide camouflage for wolves (can't tell which villagers are real)

**Weakness**: You have zero information and pure voting power. If wolves are charismatic, they can convince villagers to vote out each other.

## 💜 NEUTRAL TEAM ROLES (Win Alone)

### 🤡 Jester
**Alignment**: Neutral | **Night Order**: N/A  
**Short**: Win if voted out during day phase.

**LONG DESCRIPTION**:
You are a trickster/fool who WANTS to be voted out! Your entire goal is different from village and evil: **You WIN if you are ELIMINATED by day voting.**

**Win Condition**: Get voted out during day phase → You immediately win and are removed from game

**Night Power**: None, you sleep

**Day Power**: You can vote like normal, or you can hint that you want to be eliminated. Some versions let Jesters claim openly: "I'm the Jester, vote me out!"

**Strategy**:
- Act suspicious during day phase
- Make bold claims that sound lying ("I'm the Seer and Seat 5 is a Werewolf!") ← If false, you look bad
- Be contrarian and annoying (make people want to vote you out)
- Throw votes against the consensus
- If it's down to 1v1 (Werewolf vs Village), the Werewolf might vote you out just to spite the village

**Example Game**:
- Day 1: Jester claims "I'm Seer and Seat 3 is Werewolf!" (LYING)
- Seat 3 says "No I'm not!" and votes for Jester
- Majority votes for Jester → **JESTER WINS IMMEDIATELY!**
- Jester is removed and announces their victory
- Game continues with remaining 8 players (but Jester is done, they won!)

**Strength**: Can win from nowhere, disrupt evil/village balance  
**Weakness**: If you're TOO obvious, village will avoid voting you to prevent your win

## 🎯 EXAMPLE 9-PLAYER GAME: Alpha Wolf Setup

### Player Lineup:
1. **Alice** = Werewolf
2. **Bob** = Alpha Wolf 👑🐺
3. **Charlie** = Minion 🤝
4. **Diana** = Seer 🔍
5. **Eve** = Doctor ⚕️
6. **Frank** = Hunter 🏹
7. **Grace** = Bodyguard 🛡️
8. **Henry** = Mason 🪨
9. **Iris** = Jester 🤡

### Night 1:
- **1:00 AM**: Werewolves (Alice, Bob, Charlie—wait, Charlie is Minion!) 
  - Actually: Werewolves (Alice, Bob) + Minion (Charlie) learns who they are
  - Alice & Bob vote to kill → Kill Frank (Hunter)
- **1:15 AM**: Alpha Wolf (Bob) considers: "Should I convert someone tonight or save for later?"
- **1:30 AM**: Minion (Charlie) reports: "Alice is Werewolf #1, Bob is Werewolf #2 (Alpha Wolf)"
- **2:00 AM**: Sorceress checks Diana → "YES, SEER!"
- **2:15 AM**: Seer (Diana) checks Bob → "Bob is Alpha Wolf!"
- **2:30 AM**: Bodyguard (Grace) protects Diana (Seer)
- **2:45 AM**: Doctor (Eve) protects Diana too (double protection!)
- **3:00 AM**: Mason (Henry) learns... no other Mason in game, so nothing

### Day 1:
- **Morning**: "Frank is dead! (Hunter killed by werewolves)"
- Frank shouts before dying: "I'm Hunter! I take Seat 2 (Bob) with me!" 
  - Bob is ALSO eliminated!
- **Now: 7 players left** (Alice, Charlie, Diana, Eve, Grace, Henry, Iris)

### Village Wins!
- Killed Bob (Alpha Wolf) → Wolves are weakened
- Killed Frank (Hunter) → But got a wolf in exchange (fair trade)

## 💡 Key Tips for This Setup

**For Evil (Alice, Bob/Alpha Wolf, Charlie/Minion)**:
1. Alpha Wolf should use conversion EARLY to gain advantage
2. Minion must protect the wolves during day voting
3. Sorceress helps by finding and eliminating Seer

**For Village (Diana/Seer, Eve/Doctor, Frank/Hunter, Grace/Bodyguard, Henry/Mason)**:
1. Seer finds werewolves → Guide day votes
2. Doctor protects Seer (highest priority!)
3. Hunter baits wolves into killing them (revenge!)
4. Bodyguard guards Seer or Hunters
5. Mason finds their partner if one exists

**For Neutral (Iris/Jester)**:
1. Act suspicious to get voted out
2. Make contradictory claims
3. Try to win by day elimination!
